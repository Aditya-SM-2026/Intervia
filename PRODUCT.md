# Intervia (Medhas Academy): product context

## Register
product

## Users and purpose
- **Recruiters and hiring managers.** They are mostly in India, often reviewing several candidates in a row on a laptop.
- **What they need from a report:** decide in about a minute whether to advance a candidate, and know which moments of the recording to watch before deciding.
- **Candidates** take a live AI voice interview on the navy public pages. They never see the report.

## Brand
- Medhas Academy name and logo everywhere; never "Sreemedhas Agent".
- **Feel:** calm, trustworthy, plain-spoken. It's an assistant to a recruiter's judgement, not a verdict machine.
- **Recruiter app:** light (dark supported), using the Tailwind tokens `accent`, `surface`, `border`, `muted`.
- **Candidate pages:** deep navy `#08123a`.
- **Score colours:** emerald for 70 and above, amber for 50 and above, rose below 50.
- One sans family. No serif.

## Principles (set by the product owner)
- **Visual and outcome-first:** what does this mean for the hire, before any detail.
- **No walls of text.** Short sentence-case copy. Detail sits one tap away (expandable), never stacked by default.
- No redundant controls and no UI bugs.
- **Explain outcomes, not mechanics.** No technical terms, and no "where the eyes pointed" charts for HR.
- **Every finding links to the moment to watch.**

## Anti-references
- **Paragraphs of model-written prose** dumped into cards.
- **Rows of same-weight tinted tiles** where nothing leads.
- **Repeated information:** the same finding said in a summary, a tile and a list.
- **Jargon:** correlations, percentages of frames, gaze directions.

## Accessibility
- WCAG AA contrast, including muted text on tinted backgrounds.
- Keyboard-reachable controls, and the reduced-motion preference respected.
- Phones: layouts hold at 375px with no horizontal page scroll.
